# Timeline, Map and Knowledge Graph

## Timeline & Map
- **Timeline** – bars show how busy each day was, coloured by kind of event (calls, money, meetings, vehicles, places). Rows show when things happened in each FIR. Filter by category, FIR or text. Click an event to see details and jump to the person or number in the graph.
- **Map** – works without internet (a built-in outline of the region and an offline list of Indian places). Coloured dots are single events; numbered circles group nearby events, click to zoom. Dots placed from a place name only are approximate. Use the slider or Play to replay events over time. Street tiles can be switched on if the computer is online.
- **Compare FIRs** – two FIRs side by side; shared phones, vehicles and accounts are highlighted.
- **Gaps** – long silences between events that may need explaining.
Times are shown in your local time.

## Knowledge Graph
- **Neighbourhood** – enter an entity ID to see everything within 1–3 hops. Click a circle for details.
- **Paths** – the shortest links between two entities.
- **Communities & centrality** – groups that are tightly linked, and who sits at the centre or acts as a bridge.
- **Entity resolution** – records that may be the same person; merging changes data and needs supervisor rights.
A line means "these two appear together in the records". It is not proof of wrongdoing.
