# Who can do what, and how to get an account

## The six roles
- **Administrator** – runs the system: creates users, changes roles, gives access to cases, switches demo data on/off, makes backups, reads the whole audit log.
- **Supervisor** – does everything an investigator does and can also change data: upload FIRs, edit records, create cases, assign tasks, write notes, approve requests. First to review new access requests.
- **Investigator** – investigates and verifies. Can run every analysis, read documents, accept or reject extracted facts and confirm or dismiss a person of interest. Cannot change case records or upload.
- **Auditor** – read-only, personal details hidden. Reads the audit log and the management dashboard.
- **Demo** – a practice account. It only ever sees the built-in demo data and can switch that data on or off.

## Getting an account (there is no self sign-up)
1. On the front page choose **Request access** and fill in the form (name, official email, designation, the access you need, a reason and a password).
2. A **supervisor** sees the request under *Access Requests* and approves or rejects it, with a note.
3. Only after the supervisor approves can an **administrator** create the account (choosing the final role) or reject the request.
4. You can then sign in with the Officer ID and password you chose. Every step is recorded in the audit log.

## Demo data
The **Demo data** switch in the top bar (visible to the demo account and administrators) loads or removes the built-in practice cases. Turning it off removes only demo records; real cases are never touched. Real users do not see demo cases.

## Passwords
The demonstration accounts use simple passwords (see DEMO_ACCOUNTS.md). This is for demonstrations only; change them before real use.
